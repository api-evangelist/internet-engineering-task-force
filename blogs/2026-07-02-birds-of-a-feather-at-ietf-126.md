The IETF 126 Vienna meeting takes place 18–24 July 2026.. As at every IETF meeting, alongside the established Working Groups there will be a handful of Birds-of-a-Feather (BoF) sessions—and these are often the most interesting place to watch where the Internet's standards work is heading next.
